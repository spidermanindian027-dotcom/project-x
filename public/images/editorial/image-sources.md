## 4. Brand Color Compliance
All imagery selected strictly adheres to:
- **No** electric neons, hot magentas, or bright synthetic saturations.
- **Warm neutral foundations**: Alabaster, warm porcelain, camel, umber, and mineral black.
- **Natural lighting**: High clarity, soft directional daylight, and editorial depth of field.
